## Actor
Student paying for an accepted Direct Request; Teacher (sees the same agreed price on their order).

## Problem
DEC-02 (decided 2026-09-15) allows a teacher to accept a Direct Request at a final price different from the listed Teacher
Offering price, provided the student sees the final amount before paying. When it differs, the request, order and checkout
must show the listed price, the agreed price and the final amount.

Today:
- **Checkout mislabels the agreed price.** It shows the order price under «السعر المدرج» / "Listed price"
  (`payment-page.component.html:48-50`, key `pay_context_price`; the line is `order.price`, `payable.ts:78`).
- **No comparison anywhere.** The order detail shows "Price" and "Total" only (`order-detail-page.component.html:133-134`);
  the request detail shows only the budget (`request-detail-page.component.html:118`).
- **The listed price cannot be recovered after acceptance.**
  - Teacher Offering prices are updated **in place** (`MarketplaceService.UpdateServiceAsync` → `TeacherService.Configure`,
    `Marketplace.cs:197-210`).
  - Neither `LearningRequest` (`Orders.cs:106-108`: only budget fields) nor `Order` (`Price` = the agreed price,
    `Orders.cs:392`) stores the price the student saw when requesting.
  - `CreateLearningRequest` validates the offering but does not snapshot its price.
  - So "listed price" at payment time can only be the offering's *current* price, which the teacher may have changed since.

## User goal
As a student, I want to see clearly that the teacher proposed a different price after reading my request — what was listed,
what they propose, and the total I will pay — before I decide to pay.

## Business rule
- [Contract §3.2 "Accepted price — DECIDED (DEC-02)"](../../product/TAFSEEL_PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#32-teacher-offering): the final
  price may differ from the listed Teacher Offering price inside the Admin Catalog Service policy; the student sees the final
  amount before paying; the student is **not charged unless they explicitly proceed with payment** (acceptance of the new price
  = paying); the budget is guidance.
- §3.6/§3.7: order total = agreed price + student fee (snapshotted `studentFeePercent`, 8% today); either participant may cancel
  before payment.
- Applies to **Direct Request orders only**. Open-request orders are priced by the Teacher Offer the student chose (§3.4) — there
  is no listed price to compare.
- It is never described as an automatic price increase; the wording is neutral whether the agreed price is higher or lower.
- **DECISION REQUIRED — DEC-13: which "listed price" is authoritative.** See Dependencies. Until decided, Gate 3 is blocked.

## Business states
| Order (direct, `awaiting payment + pending`) | Disclosure |
|----------------------------------------------|------------|
| agreed price = listed price | No comparison; price, fee, total as today (with the corrected label «السعر») |
| agreed price ≠ listed price | Comparison panel on request detail, order detail and checkout |
| listed price unknown (requests created before the snapshot, or DEC-13 option not providing it) | No comparison; label «السعر الذي اقترحه المعلم» + fee + total |
| order paid / completed | Order detail keeps showing listed vs agreed for the record; checkout not reachable |

## Preconditions
- DEC-13 decided.
- If option A: listed price snapshot is available on the request/order responses.

## Happy path
1. Student requests a teacher's «شرح مسجّل» offering listed at 100 SAR.
2. Teacher accepts at 150 SAR (inside policy).
3. Student opens the request (notification «طلبك مقبول — أكمل الدفع») → sees the panel: listed 100, proposed 150, fee 12, total 162,
   and «ادفع الآن».
4. Student opens checkout → sees the same four lines above the pay button (and in the sticky mobile total: 162).
5. Student pays → payment amount 162; order detail keeps "listed 100 · agreed 150".

## Negative cases
| Case | What the student sees |
|------|------------------------|
| Student does not want the new price | «راسل المعلم» / "Message the teacher" and «إلغاء الطلب» / "Cancel the order" on the order screen (existing, before payment); nothing charged |
| Teacher changed the offering price after the request | With option A: still the snapshot the student saw. With option B: the current price (the mislabel risk DEC-13 describes) |
| Currency of listed and agreed differ | Not possible in V1 (SAR only); if it occurs, no comparison is shown |
| Price outside policy | Refused at acceptance by the server (`service_price_out_of_policy`) — never reaches the student |

## Authorization
Unchanged. Student and teacher of the order see the order's prices; the teacher sees the same listed/agreed comparison on
their order detail (never the student's payment details); other users 404.

## UX
- **Entry point:** request detail `/requests/:id` (accepted direct request, next-step panel); order detail `/orders/:id`;
  checkout `/checkout?orderId=`; reached from Student home card A5 (`UX-01`) and the "PaymentRequired" notification.
- **Information hierarchy (price panel, same on all three screens):**
  1. Title «السعر الذي اقترحه المعلم بعد مراجعة طلبك» / "The price the teacher proposed after reviewing your request".
  2. «السعر المعروض في ملف المعلم» / "Price listed on the teacher's profile" — {listed} (secondary text).
  3. «السعر الذي اقترحه المعلم» / "Price the teacher proposed" — {agreed} (emphasis).
  4. «رسوم خدمة تفصيل ({percent}٪)» / "Tafseel service fee ({percent}%)" — {studentFeeAmount}.
  5. «المبلغ الإجمالي» / "Total to pay" — {studentTotal} (largest).
  6. Note: «لن يُخصم أي مبلغ إلا إذا اخترت الدفع. يمكنك مراسلة المعلم أو إلغاء الطلب قبل الدفع.» / "Nothing is charged unless you
     choose to pay. You can message the teacher or cancel before paying."
- **Primary CTA per state:** unpaid: «ادفع الآن» / "Pay now" (request detail → checkout; order detail → checkout; checkout → pay);
  paid: none.
- **Wording (AR / EN):** above. Corrections:
  - `pay_context_price` «السعر المدرج» / "Listed price" → «السعر المتفق عليه» / "Agreed price" when showing `order.price`;
  - the order detail `demand_price` label becomes «السعر المتفق عليه» / "Agreed price" for direct orders;
  - forbidden words: «زيادة», «رفع السعر», "increase", "surcharge".
- **Mobile (390px):**
  - the panel is a two-column label/amount list (amount aligned to the end), wrapping labels;
  - checkout sticky bar shows «المبلغ الإجمالي {studentTotal}» + «ادفع الآن»;
  - the comparison stays in the scrolling receipt above;
  - no horizontal scroll.
- **Loading / empty / error / not-available states:** inherits each screen's states; unknown listed price → no comparison row
  (never "0" or "—").
- **Confirmation dialogs:** none new. The checkout pay button — labelled with the total in the sticky bar — is the student's
  explicit acceptance; the price panel sits directly above it.
- **Gate question:** yes — the student sees in plain words what was listed, what the teacher proposes and what they will pay,
  and that nothing happens unless they pay.

matching `teacherServiceId` and shows its current `price` as "listed".

Error codes the UI must explain: none new (`service_price_out_of_policy` is the teacher's, at acceptance).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AC1 For a direct order with agreed ≠ listed, request detail, order detail and checkout show listed, agreed, fee (with snapshotted percent) and total, with the AR/EN copy above, before any payment.
- [ ] AC2 For agreed = listed or unknown listed price, no comparison row is shown; labels read "Agreed price"/«السعر المتفق عليه» (checkout no longer says "Listed price" for the agreed price).
- [ ] AC3 No "increase" wording in either language; the note says nothing is charged unless the student pays.
- [ ] AC4 Paying charges exactly `studentTotal`; the student is never charged without pressing pay.
- [ ] AC5 Open-request orders show no listed-price comparison.
- [ ] AC6 (Option A) The listed price is the offering price at request creation even if the teacher changes the offering afterwards.

## Out of scope
- Negotiation or counter-offers; showing the student's budget in the panel; open-request offers; VAT wording (`DEC-08`).
- Changing fee rules or order financial snapshots.

## Dependencies
- **DEC-13 — Listed price reference for the agreed-price disclosure (OPEN, blocks Gate 3).**
  - **A. Snapshot (recommended).** Store the offering price on the Direct Request at creation. Accurate: shows exactly what the
    student saw. Touches protected `Tafseel.Domain` (`LearningRequest`) and adds a migration, so the Product Owner must authorize
    the protected-code change. Requests created before the change show no comparison.
  - **B. Current price.** Compare against the teacher's current offering price at view time. No backend change, but it
    misleads when the teacher edits the offering between request and payment (the comparison can show a change that never
    happened, or hide one).
  - **C. No comparison.** Show only "the price the teacher proposed" and the total. No backend change, but it does not meet DEC-02
    as recorded ("show the listed price").
- `UX-04` (vocabulary) — build dependency.
