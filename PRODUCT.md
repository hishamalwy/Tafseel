# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Tafseel serves Students seeking educational help, Teachers offering qualified services, Quality Reviewers governing teaching evidence, and Admins operating the marketplace. Marketplace Intelligence primarily serves Admins who need to understand demand, supply, funnel health, zero-result discovery, and transactional outcomes over bounded periods.

## Product Purpose

Tafseel is an educational marketplace that moves legitimate Student needs through Teacher discovery, contextual services, requests or live sessions, payment, delivery, completion, and review. Success means those journeys remain truthful, safe, understandable, and operationally measurable from first-party evidence.

## Positioning

Marketplace behavior and internal intelligence derive from canonical qualifications, eligible public offers, lifecycle persistence, and minimal first-party interaction events. Tafseel does not substitute opaque ranking, fabricated metrics, or AI inference for business truth.

## Operating Context

Public discovery and Teacher Profiles lead into Guided Requests or live scheduling. Teachers accept requests and fulfil Orders; Students pay, review deliveries, complete Orders, and submit eligible Reviews. Admins and Quality Reviewers use role-specific dashboards for operations and governance. Release reporting and certification are evidence-first and retain historical conclusions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Canonical public Teacher eligibility, Subjects, Service Catalog, TeacherServices, contextual prices, Reviews, availability, Favorites, Requests, Orders, Payments, Deliveries, Revisions, Live Sessions, notifications, and Order communication already exist.
- Internal Marketplace Intelligence may calculate explicit metrics from persisted truth and necessary append-only interaction events.
- F-002 prohibits unsupported public Teacher performance metrics.
- No Teacher ranking, recommendation, AI, advertising analytics, invasive tracking, or raw search-text persistence.
- Analytics must be privacy-minimized, bounded, idempotent where client retries are possible, and unable to manufacture transactional truth.
- Analytics transport failure must never block product journeys.
- English/LTR and Arabic/RTL operational UX are required.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Tafseel. Existing Tafseel visual identity, bilingual terminology, navigation, tokens, and role-aware dashboard patterns remain authoritative. Operational copy must be factual, calm, and explicit about formulas, periods, missing data, and partial historical coverage.

## Evidence on Hand

Canonical repository entities and histories provide transactional evidence. Release 6 supplies deterministic discovery and contextual Subject/Service routing. Existing audit, notification, outbox, integration tests, browser harnesses, and release reports are evidence sources. No customer claims, public benchmarks, ranking criteria, or AI-derived facts may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Canonical business truth outranks telemetry.
2. Instrument only interactions that answer a named product question.
3. Make formulas, denominators, periods, and coverage limits visible.
4. Keep internal intelligence separate from public Teacher reputation.
5. Prefer the smallest first-party architecture that remains secure and scalable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Operational surfaces must support keyboard use, semantic labels, textual equivalents for charts, non-color-only meaning, responsive 390–1440px layouts, and complete English/LTR and Arabic/RTL behavior.
